PhilHealth YAKAP Registration & Maintenance Meds

When a patient wishes to secure maintenance medications at Gubat RHU, the first essential step is to verify their PhilHealth eligibility and check if Gubat RHU is registered as their assigned YAKAP Clinic Provider. If no YAKAP clinic is selected upon checking their eligibility claims, any resident of Gubat seeking Yakap Gamot benefits is required to officially register Gubat RHU as their designated primary care provider before receiving their prescription.

Primary Care Selection Form (PCSF)

The official form used by PhilHealth members to choose or update their designated Primary Care Clinic (PCC) under the PhilHealth YAKAP primary care benefit package. This form is specifically utilized at Gubat RHU for manual empanelment, catering to patients who do not possess cellphones, active mobile numbers, or email addresses for online registration.

PhilHealth Member Registration Form (PMRF)

The mandatory registration document for individuals who do not currently have an active PhilHealth membership but wish to enroll. To successfully secure a PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN) and access healthcare benefits, the applicant must complete the PMRF and submit all accompanying documentary requirements listed on the form.
